Publication safeguard

Faculty uploads never become public automatically. Approval and publication are separate recorded actions. Withdrawing publication removes public access while preserving the secure file, version history, review notes, and audit trail.

Institutional operation

Use named accounts, strong passwords, periodic backups, storage monitoring, and a formal privacy review. The Railway persistent volume must remain attached at /data so uploaded documents and portal records survive deployments.
